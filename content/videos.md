# Video lectures

Every topic of the CS-401T syllabus, in syllabus order, with YouTube lectures that explain it. Watch the short one before reading the unit notes; use the longer one when a topic still feels shaky.

> **How to use this page:** tap a thumbnail to play it here. 5 Minutes Engineering and Gate Smashers (Hindi) and Mahesh Huddar (English, slow board work) cover the exam topics and the numericals (k-NN, ID3, perceptron, backpropagation). StatQuest and 3Blue1Brown give the clearest pictures of gradient descent, backpropagation and SVM. For the Unit 4 learning theory, which Indian channels rarely teach, the lectures by Shai Ben-David (co-author of the Shalev-Shwartz and Ben-David textbook) are linked. Topics marked ⭐ are the ones MJPRU has asked; see the [PYQ analysis](#analysis).
